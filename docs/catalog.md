# Parameter catalog

Generated by `synth_catalog` from the DSP descriptor tables: do not edit by hand. Machine-readable
version: `docs/catalog.json` (open format OID 0.3, `spec/README.md`).

Patch values are in **physical units**. **live** = safe to move while playing (CC, modulation);
the CC number is the default assignment. **role** = meaning in the shared vocabulary
(`spec/roles.json`): a patch may use a role instead of a name. Patch format version: 1.

## Engine `drone` (8 voices)

drone machine: each note an endless drone of wandering harmonic partials, a warm body of saws, air on the harmonics and rare sparkles; temperature (glacial .. warm), density, motion, depth, brightness, sparkle, width; envelopes up to 3 minutes (8 voices)

| name | range / choices | default | live | role | what it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `temperature` | 0 .. 1 | 0.5 | yes | `drone.temperature` | glacial (0: pure glassy partials, high whistling air, bright bells) .. warm (1: dark partials, a body of detuned saws, low breathy air) |
| `density` | 0 .. 1 | 0.5 | yes | `drone.density` | how many partials sound (1 .. 12) and how much air: a lone sine .. a wall |
